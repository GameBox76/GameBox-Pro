How to play

  • Drag or move the pointer to fly. Arrow keys or WASD also work. Your ship fires on its own.
  • Kill fast to build a chain. Chains of 5, 10, 15 and 20 raise the score multiplier up to ×5.
  • 🔱 spread shot, 🛡️ shield, ⚡ rapid fire, 💣 screen bomb, 💚 repair.
  • A boss arrives every 5th wave and gets angrier at half health.
  • Press P or Esc to pause.
